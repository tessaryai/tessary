## The summary field

One sentence, under 320 characters: what moved, and what that means for whoever reads it.
Lead with the finding, not with your method. Everything you weighed is already on the screen
beside this sentence — the dossier is what a reader opens next, so do not restate it, and do
not narrate your own reasoning. If your ruling is `unclear`, the sentence says what would
settle it.

If it is `blocked`, the sentence names what was unreachable and which call failed.
